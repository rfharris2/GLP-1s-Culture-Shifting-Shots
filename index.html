GLP-1s - Culture Shifting Shots!

A Breakthrough, or the Next Fad?

GLP-1s are proving they can genuinely change lives—but whether they're a lasting medical breakthrough or just the next health fad depends on factors we don't have the full picture on yet. We're still figuring out how safe they really are long-term, who can actually access them, and whether people can use them without it backfiring. Lap-Bands were sold as the perfect solution for weight loss that were going to help everyone, but a couple decades later, we know that they have less than a 20% success rate. On the other side, Viagra began its life as a simple blood pressure treatment but went on to make huge, lasting waves. What fate will GLP-1s have in the long term? My goal is that by the end of this project, I will be able to make an educated argument for one side or the other.

The Capture Rule:

Is the GLP-1 phenomenon the beginning of a paradigm shift or are we in the middle of a fad that will eventually fade away?

My goal throughout this project is to be exposed to a variety of both scientific and anecdotal evidence to form as broad and as clear a picture as possible. If it makes a factual claim about GLP-1 outcomes (efficacy, side effects, long-term use, behavior, economics) and isn't trying to sell something, it will be worth capturing, regardless of which side it argues. Things that may almost count but don't would be advertisements for GLP-1s, anecdotes with no numbers behind them ("my cousin lost 40 pounds and felt amazing") as these are really just data points about individuals, not necessarily the trend. Think-pieces and cultural commentary ("GLP-1s and the end of diet culture") almost count but are opinions about meaning, not evidence about the drug. They may be worth capturing for the "paradigm shift" half of my question, but not as scientific evidence. I'm going to absorb as much as I can but I will refrain from capturing evidence that is explicitly biased.

We won't know the real economic effect until we have answers to all three, not just the first one.
