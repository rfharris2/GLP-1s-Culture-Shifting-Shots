GLP-1s - Culture Shifting Shots!

A Breakthrough, or the Next Fad?

GLP-1s are proving they can genuinely change lives—but whether they're a lasting medical breakthrough or just the next health fad depends on factors we don't have the full picture on yet. We're still figuring out how safe they really are long-term, who can actually access them, and whether people can use them without it backfiring. Lap-Bands were sold as the perfect solution for weight loss that were going to help everyone, but a couple decades later, we know that they have less than a 20% success rate. On the other side, Viagra began its life as a simple blood pressure treatment but went on to make huge, lasting waves. What fate will GLP-1s have in the long term? My goal is that by the end of this project, I will be able to make an educated argument for one side or the other.

This WSJ podcast episode, "How Big Food Is Navigating the GLP-1 Era," highlights how food companies are being forced to rethink their entire strategy. For decades, the industry leaned into sugar, salt, and flavor to drive sales — but with GLP-1s and health-conscious shopping on the rise, that playbook is being challenged. It's a good example of how this isn't just a personal health story, but one that's reshaping entire industries from the top down.
This Instagram post cites McKinsey data showing just how quickly grocery spending shifts once someone starts a GLP-1: within 6 months, purchases of chips, sweet bakery items, cookies, and soft drinks all dropped by 7-11%, while yogurt and fresh produce rose. What stands out to me is how granular this data is — it's not just "people eat less," it's a full-scale reordering of the grocery cart. This feels like strong evidence that GLP-1s are reshaping the food industry from the consumer side, not just the pharmaceutical side.
